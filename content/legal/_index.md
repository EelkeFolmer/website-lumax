## Privacy

This website does not use cookies, does not show advertising and contains no tracking scripts or embedded content from other companies. Fonts and images are served from this site itself.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. To deliver the pages, Netlify's servers record technical data about each request, such as the IP address, the page requested, the time and the browser. We use these logs only in aggregate, to see which pages are visited, and not to identify visitors. Netlify processes this data on our behalf; see [Netlify's privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).

**Settings in your browser.** If you switch between the day and night theme, your choice is stored in your own browser (local storage), so that it is remembered on your next visit. It is not sent to us.

**Email.** If you email us, we use your name, address and message only to reply and, where it leads to a project, to work with you. We keep correspondence no longer than needed for that purpose and for our legal record-keeping obligations.

**Your rights.** Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask us which personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to [hello@lumax.ai](mailto:hello@lumax.ai). You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
