<div class="theme-intro">
  <div class="theme-intro-text">
    <p>Throughout our careers we have been involved in monitoring seabird colonies and developing efficient, low-disturbance survey techniques. Our pioneering work in the mid-2010s using drones demonstrated that aerial mapping provided a powerful alternative to ground-based methods.</p>
    <p>Together with the <a href="https://sovon.nl">Dutch Centre for Field Ornithology (Sovon)</a>, we built AI-BIRD: users can upload drone imagery to the cloud, where photogrammetric processing produces orthorectified maps that are then automatically analysed for the presence of dead and living birds.</p>

  <div class="theme-block-text">
    <h2>Dead bird detection</h2>
    <p>AI-BIRD includes a dedicated dead bird finder to assess mortality from avian influenza outbreaks. The model automatically identifies casualties in drone imagery, enabling rapid large-scale mortality assessments without entering the colony.</p>

    <h2>Interactive data portal</h2>
    <p>AI-BIRD is a cloud-based platform for efficient monitoring of breeding birds and detection of avian influenza casualties in drone imagery. The platform automatically detects and counts species, identifies nests, and tracks colony development over time.</p>
