Throughout our careers we have been involved in monitoring seabird colonies and developing efficient, low-disturbance survey techniques. Together with the [Dutch Centre for Field Ornithology (Sovon)](https://sovon.nl), we built AI-BIRD: users can upload drone imagery to the cloud, where photogrammetric processing produces orthorectified maps that are then automatically analysed for the presence of dead and living birds.

![AI-BIRD flu detection](https://lumax.ai/images/AIBird_flu_example_hu4582156328056167553.png)

![AI-BIRD portal](https://lumax.ai/images/AIBIRD_portal_hu11745037953255307095.png)

AI-BIRD is a cloud-based platform for efficient monitoring of breeding birds and detection of avian influenza casualties in drone imagery.
