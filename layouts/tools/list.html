Tools

Platforms and applications built by Lumax — practical tools for researchers and conservation managers.

Bird monitoring platform

AI-BIRD

AI-BIRD is a cloud-based platform for efficient monitoring of breeding bird colonies and detection of avian influenza casualties in drone imagery. Users upload drone footage to the cloud, where photogrammetry and computer vision models automatically detect and count live and dead birds — with minimal disturbance to the colony.

Built in collaboration with the Dutch Centre for Field Ornithology (Sovon), AI-BIRD is used to survey seabird colonies across the Netherlands and beyond.

Visit aibird.org

Salmon monitoring platform

SalmonVision

SalmonVision is an AI platform for automated salmon counting and species identification from underwater cameras, sonar and drones. Computer vision models detect, count and identify salmon species in real time — providing resource managers and First Nations with timely, reliable data on salmon returns.

Developed together with the Pacific Salmon Foundation, Wild Salmon Center and Simon Fraser University, and deployed across the coast of British Columbia.

Visit salmonvision.org

Data visualisation platform

CODAP

CODAP (Common Online Data Analysis Platform) is a free, open-source platform for visualising and analysing ecological data. It combines tables, charts and maps in one interactive workspace — no coding required.

Lumax uses CODAP to make coastal monitoring data — including sedimentation, erosion and vegetation data from the Wadden Sea — accessible to land stewards and resource managers. The dedicated portal is currently in development.
